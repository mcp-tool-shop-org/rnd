## Summary

The PTX version shipped with CUDA 13.4. It adds hardware-targeted instructions and the preview target for Rubin.

## Key points

- New instructions: L1 prefetch, int4-to-int8 matrix loads, packed FP8 arithmetic, lighter-weight proxy fences.
- Developer-preview target for Rubin (reported as sm_107).
- Works with the CUDA Tile features and programmatic dependent launch in 13.4.

## Studio relevance

Reaches us only through the kernels in libraries and engines (cuBLAS, llama.cpp, PyTorch). The low-precision instructions (int4, FP8) are what quantised inference builds on.
