## Summary

E4b asked whether each device keeps its speed when another runs beside it. The first complete block
(`e4b-block-2`, harness at f3f5e44) decided the two pairs that measure the 5090. A two-pair re-run (`e4b-block-3`, at
426f106) decided the other two after a harness fix. All four pairs are free. The decision rule was pre-registered: a pair counts as "free" when the 90% interval of the per-triplet
log-ratio lies inside ±0.10. The two pairs that measure the 5090 came out free: an Intel companion running beside it
changes its decode rate by under 1.4%. The two pairs that measure the NPU and the iGPU beside a 5090 companion are
inconclusive. That isn't noise. A harness defect started the Intel calls before the 5090 companion had reached a steady
decode, so those pairs were re-run after a fix, and both came out free.

## Key points

**The two pairs measuring the 5090: free.**

| Pair | HL estimate | 90% interval | Refusals | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5090 beside the NPU | −0.83% | [−1.20, −0.46]% | 0 | free |
| 5090 beside the iGPU | +0.57% | [+0.24, +1.38]% | 0 | free |

- Both intervals sit about ten times inside the ±10% band.
- They exclude zero, so the effect is real but small.

**The two pairs measuring the NPU and iGPU: inconclusive.**
- **What refused them:** every refusal was `companion_clock_range`, the 5090 companion's clock moving more than
  22.5 MHz.
- **The cause:** a paired Intel slot lasts about 100 ms, and the harness ran it at the very start of the companion's
  first generate. So the companion's reported call included the clock ramp from an idle card.
- **Consequence:** the spec asks for the companion's decode to be sustained before the Intel calls, so the completed
  triplets don't test the stated condition either. Their numbers are not results.
- **Next:** wait for one full steady warm decode on the companion, then re-run only these two pairs.

**The run itself:**
- it completed cleanly in about 30 minutes, from a clean tree;
- peak VRAM was about 12.6 GB;
- the GPU probe was NVML at a 0.206 s sample interval;
- the clock tolerance and the steady band were 22.5 MHz, set from the pilot.

**Watch items:**
- In every iGPU triplet, the alone-after slot ran about 5% faster than the alone-before slot. This is a carry-over
  step the triplet mean doesn't cancel.
- The block's first NPU slot ran slow, which is wake-up after idle.

**The re-run (`e4b-block-3`, `--pairs npu-5090,igpu-5090`).** The 5090 companion now finishes one steady warm decode
before the Intel calls start, and it is judged on the call that covers the measurement. There were no refusals and no
attempt reasons. R&D recomputed the estimates and the intervals from the receipt's d_i.

| Pair (measured device beside the 5090) | HL estimate | 90% interval | Refusals | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NPU latency | −0.61% | [−1.31, +0.06]% | 0 | free |
| iGPU latency | +2.48% | [−0.99, +4.82]% | 0 | free |

- **The iGPU interval is wider.** Its paired calls fall into two groups, about 38 ms and about 40 ms. Four triplets read
  about +6%, and one slow alone-before slot gives −9%. The upper bound of +4.8% is still well inside ±10%.
- **Watch items from block 2, resolved:**
  - the iGPU's alone-after step is gone (alone-after now matches alone-before), so it came from the companion ramp;
  - the first-NPU-slot slowness fell on the unanalysed cold slot, not in a triplet.

## Studio relevance

The interlock router can treat the 5090, the NPU and the iGPU as independent lanes. Running them together costs the 5090
under 1.4% and the Intel devices at most about 5% (the iGPU's upper bound), all inside the pre-registered ±10%. E4's
question is answered. The iGPU's occasional ~6% step beside a 5090 decode is worth knowing for latency-sensitive iGPU
work.

## Claims

- [verified] The 5090 decode rate changed by −0.83% [−1.20, −0.46] beside the NPU and +0.57% [+0.24, +1.38] beside the iGPU, under the pre-registered exact Wilcoxon interval at 0.916 (via: R&D's read of receipts/e4b-block-2.jsonl, 2026-10-12)
- [verified] Every refusal in the NPU-measured and iGPU-measured pairs was companion_clock_range, and the harness starts the Intel calls with no wait for the 5090 companion's decode (via: R&D's read of the receipt and of perform_slot at f3f5e44, 2026-10-12)
- [verified] With a sustained 5090 companion, NPU latency changed by −0.61% [−1.31, +0.06] and iGPU latency by +2.48% [−0.99, +4.82], with no refusals (via: R&D's recomputation from receipts/e4b-block-3.jsonl, 2026-10-12)
- [verified] Block 2's iGPU alone-after step does not recur with a sustained companion (via: R&D's read of receipts/e4b-block-3.jsonl, 2026-10-12)
