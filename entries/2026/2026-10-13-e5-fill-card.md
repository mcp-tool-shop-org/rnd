## Summary

E5 asks, for each local 5090 model, the largest context window (`num_ctx`) that stays fully on the card, stays under
30500 MiB, and doesn't spill into system memory. The card is now complete, from two runs:
- **`e5-2026-10-13b`** (interlock d899ac0) gave three valid answers at their trained caps.
- **`e5-2026-10-13c`** (interlock 0db35c8) re-measured the other three after a dated amendment. All three are
  capacities, not lower bounds.

The trimmed records are in rnd: `experiments/e5/results/run-b.json` and `run-c.json`, collected at pinned commits
with `rnd collect e5`.

## Key points

| Model | Largest resident window | Trained cap | How it ended | Run |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| llama3.1:8b | **131072** | 131072 | the cap; peak 23962 MiB | c |
| gemma4:31b | **86016** | 262144 | measured: 88064 peaked at 30544 MiB, 44 over the ceiling | c |
| granite4.1:30b | **43008** | 131072 | predicted: 45056 forecast over from measured slopes; 49152 measured over | c |
| qwen3:14b | **40960** | 40960 | the cap | b |
| qwen3:8b | **40960** | 40960 | the cap | b |
| mistral-small:24b | **32768** | 32768 | the cap | b |

**How the two run-c limits were judged:**
- **gemma4:31b is bracketed by a measurement.**
  - The confirm walk, which fills each window with a prompt, measured 88064 over the ceiling.
  - The load-only search had passed 92160. A load without a prompt peaks about 150 to 250 MiB lower than a filled
    confirm at the same window.
  - Neighbouring confirms wobble by about ±250 MiB, so the true edge lies between 86016 and 88064.
- **granite4.1:30b is bracketed by a prediction and a measurement.**
  - 43008 confirmed at 30386 MiB, 114 under the ceiling.
  - 45056 was forecast at 30601 MiB from the measured slope. The local slope (+523 MiB per 2k) gives about 30756,
    and a filled confirm runs higher than a load.
  - 49152 was measured over at load (31582 MiB).
  - One confirm at 45056 would settle it, if a decision ever needs it.

**What changed between the runs.** The amendment before run c:
- the shared-memory gate counts only under capacity pressure, at or above 28452 MiB peak;
- the prompt-rate gate is a cliff against the next lower confirmed window, not against the floor;
- the predictor uses measured peak-per-token slopes, which fixed run b's full-attention over-prediction for
  sliding-window (gemma) and hybrid (granite) models.

In run c:
- shared memory rose at most 83 MiB over the floor for gemma and granite, and 271 MiB for llama3.1:8b at 131072,
  which is host-side scaling, not a spill;
- the prompt rate fell smoothly with window size, with no cliff;
- every floor passed the CUDA_Host check.

**How spill is measured:** each load reserves pinned host buffers (CUDA_Host) that Windows counts as shared GPU
memory: about 352 MiB for qwen3:8b, and 1128 MiB for gemma4:31b, whose large vocabulary embedding sits on the host.
So the reference is the model's own floor window, not the idle card.

**Defects found in run c's receipt** (none changes a result; queued as one interlock job):
- **gemma's `limit` reads `predicted` but should read `margin`.** The confirm walk stops on a measured abort without
  updating the label, contrary to the harness's own docstring.
- **granite's first confirm (4096) peaked at 27329 MiB**, about 5.7 GB over its neighbours. It ran straight after the
  43008 load, so the sampler probably caught that runner still freeing memory. Near the ceiling, that could cause a
  false abort. The remedy is to wait for the card to return to idle before each measured call.
- **`decode_tok_s` reads 1,000,000 tok/s** because each confirm generates one token. The collected records leave it
  out.

Run b's misfire and defects are in this entry's history and in [[2026-10-12-test-fakes-hide-the-platform]].

## Studio relevance

For routing on the 5090:
- llama3.1:8b, qwen3:8b, qwen3:14b and mistral-small:24b can all use their full trained windows.
- gemma4:31b holds about 86k tokens of its 262k: a third of its window, but the largest absolute context on the card.
- granite4.1:30b holds 43k of its 131k.

A router that sends a long-context job to gemma4:31b must cap `num_ctx` at 86016 (or 88064 at most, at the run-to-run
edge), or the job spills into system memory.

Each figure holds for Ollama 0.35.1, driver 617.42, f16 KV cache with flash attention, Q4_K_M weights and an idle card.
A quantised KV cache would raise the gemma and granite figures; that is a separate experiment.
