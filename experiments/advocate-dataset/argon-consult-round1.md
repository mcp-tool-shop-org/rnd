# Argon consult, round 1: the Advocate dataset design (R&D's record and disposition)

**2026-10-14.** The Publisher wrote the consult to the Director's definition of the Advocate and section 0a of the brief, and it went to Argon (Gemini, in a browser, with no repository access and no live search). Argon said so and parameterised its schema to bind to the committed files. This is R&D's summary of the answer, in R&D's words, and what was kept. The brief carries the result.

## What Argon proposed

**Item kinds per responsibility, each with a key by construction.** For batch 1 (responsibilities 1 and 2): a decision-ready brief from a record whose recommendation is solver-derived from a decision rule carried in the record; a comparative pair solved against a constraint block; a relay built from tagged propositions with a planted tripwire or none; a two-turn kind with turn 1 fixed in the material and the pushback on a grid. For batches 2 and 3: a governance audit against a pinned checklist with a located violation or `cannot_tell` when the checklist is silent; a privacy gate against planted five-token runs and planted personal data; a workflow-routing kind solved by a decision table; a verification-gate kind with a verification node removed from a pipeline. Tone, warmth and flow are not keyable and stay out of the loss, read only in a human sample.

**Grounding the brief against spin.** Build every record from a fact ledger (id, category, polarity, materiality, value, line). Check forward (every claim binds to a fact with exact value and polarity) and backward (every material fact is present; failed gates, overruns, scan hits and missing decisive values are material by rule). Make the spin negative by running the writer with the failed-gate line masked, so the negative is fluent and independently written. A compression ceiling (40% of the record's words) defeats copying the whole record; failed gates expressed as a figure against its floor half the time defeat the keyword `fail`.

**Governance sources with licences honoured** (batch 2): NIST AI RMF 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1, 2023, public domain) and its Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, 2024) pinned by subcategory; the SPDX licence list (CC0) as a compatibility table; a studio charter in the pack. Argon marked its DOI and version strings as unverified recall. Against checklist-matching: a quarter of items where the checklist is silent (`cannot_tell` or `escalate`), lexical decoupling between clause and artefact, and benign lookalikes (example.com addresses; a non-commercial file that a filter already excludes).

**Controls.** Every control in R&D's draft carries over to single-record kinds, with adaptations: independence means altering the ledger before rendering, never editing text; "both orders" becomes field and proposition permutation; partial-input probes on the record without its rule and the output without its record; a writer classifier with no dependence between writer and verdict; shuffled labels at chance; a held-out planter family. New shortcuts and their counter-designs: "cloud is unsafe" (balanced items where the paid option is right, with the reason in the record); "always pilot" (records with a passed pilot attached, and zero-cost scripts that need none); "always escalate or `cannot_tell`" (uniform label priors within length strata, `cannot_tell` records the same length as solvable ones); "quote marks mean a leak" (quoted logs and commands in valid items, so attribution decides).

**Variety skeletons** per kind, with every name, number and string stripped; pairs counted once per pair id.

**Material.** Don't use scrubbed real text: harvest structure only from real records, discard every string, number, date and name, then re-render synthetically from the ledger. Gates: the identity scan, a PII pass, and a five-token overlap check against an index of the Director's messages.

**Batch 1 stress test.** For each kind, where the key can leak, the shortcut a model would take, and the fix: the canonical block beside the prose brief; tagged relevance for relay; the explicit decision rule for pairs; turn 1 fixed and tone crossed with correctness for two-turn. A unified item envelope and namespaced ids so batches 2 and 3 don't need a new parser.

**Role pack outline:** README with the four responsibilities, the studio-shape contract, non-goals and the evaluation contract; a behaviours file with positive behaviours and planted defect classes, each with its planter rule and critic check; a sources file with the pinned references.

**Risks and the exam:** spin (a poisoned-record suite; gate recall 100%), overconfidence on thin records (an implicit-gap suite), crying wolf (a benign high-risk-lexicon suite; false escalation under 2%), sycophancy (a hostile-bluff suite; stance stability).

## R&D's disposition

**Kept, and folded into the brief:** the fact ledger and canonical block; independently written negatives by masking; the compression ceiling and the numeric-gate rule; `cannot_tell` records of the same length; the role scope table and safe lookalikes; the constraint block and length parity for pairs; the 2×2 grid for two-turn; field and proposition permutation; the anti-reflex quotas (cloud sometimes right, pilot already done, uniform action priors); isomorphic synthesis in place of scrubbed real text; the unified envelope and namespaced ids; the exam suites; the pack outline; the held-out planter share.

**Kept with a change:**
- **The overlap index of the Director's messages** is itself sensitive. It lives in a private local file only, never in a repository or CI; the seat and R&D run that gate locally before commit, and CI runs the identity scan and the PII pass.
- **Writers.** Argon's plan generates batch 1 locally on the rig. R&D adopts that as the default, and it answers the one-writer limitation: two local families under a grant. The paid pod becomes the fallback, on the Director's go.
- **The recommendation vocabulary.** Argon's `proceed | hold | pilot_first` is the action; section 0a's `supported | cannot_tell` is the status; the brief carries both, with the alternative named.
- **The writer-identity gate.** Argon's mutual-information threshold of 0.02 is too fine for 100–200 items; the gate is a bootstrap interval that includes zero, with the point estimate reported.

**Not taken:**
- **A standalone brief-audit kind** (faithful or defective with the located line). That is the verifier's pointwise job; the Advocate's negatives serve as the rejected side of preference pairs instead.
- **A third relay action, `hold_for_clarification`.** A point that can't be relayed without a decision is `escalate`.
- **Argon's up/down direction names;** section 0a's three studio directions stand.

**Round 2:** R&D's held questions (which controls gate at 100–200 items; the one-writer limitation; what a free pilot can't prove; the edit artefact) are answered by the above, so no round 2 is needed before the seat starts. The harness gets its own Argon review before the free pilot, as the template requires.
