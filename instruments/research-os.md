## Summary

The heavy instrument. Turns a topic into a research-pack repo with append-only ledgers, deterministic excerpt IDs (models cite spans, never author them), contradiction mapping and readiness gates. Frozen packs are archived in mcp-tool-shop-org/research-packs.

## Studio relevance

When a library entry here grows into a topic that a decision will depend on, graduate it into a research-os pack and link the pack from the entry.
